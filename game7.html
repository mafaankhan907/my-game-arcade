🥤 Poison Drinks

2 Player Survival Game

🔵 Player 1

Health: 100

Poison: 0/3

🔴 Player 2

Health: 100

Poison: 0/3

Player 1's Turn
Choose a drink!

🎮 How to Play

There are 10 drinks. 3 contain poison. Choose a drink on your turn. Drinking poison damages your health. Get poisoned 3 times and you lose!
